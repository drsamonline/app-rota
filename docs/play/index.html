The team and the days

The rota

A clash blocks the save. A rest-day violation is flagged and does not: a team may deliberately run someone seven days, but nobody deliberately puts two people on one shift.

Add people to a slot with the small menu under it. A slot that ends up with two people is a clash, and a rota with a clash cannot be saved — the mistake is shown rather than swallowed.
